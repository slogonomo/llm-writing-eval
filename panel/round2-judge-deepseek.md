## Scores

### Entry A
- **Skill adherence: 9** — All 15 beats present, concrete, and correctly marked for 110 pages; the need-arc lands at Midpoint, All Is Lost, and Final Image; the assessment names Fun and Games as the weak beat and prescribes the fix (each world must cost Nia something concrete).
- **Emotive: 9** — Restrained, precise writing ("the worst hurt comes from people she loves") that earns the abandonment symmetry without spelling it out.
- **Creativity: 9** — Confident, specific texture: a translation interface refined since age eight, the 2098 sentience ruling, infrastructure built for traveling cetaceans, and a granddaughter's pregnancy as the matriline grace note.

### Entry B
- **Skill adherence: 8** — Complete and correctly named, with accurate markers and a working arc, but All Is Lost leans external rather than internal, and the sheet's own assessment flags the Break into Two flood-override as a contrivance risk.
- **Emotive: 7** — Competent and event-driven; emotion is reported ("she sees that her rush brought an armed fleet down") more than felt.
- **Creativity: 7** — Plays the shared premise's beats straight; the orcas triggering the flood override is the one fresh mechanism.

### Entry C
- **Skill adherence: 9** — Supplies the skill's full input block (logline, want/need, staged antagonist), all 15 beats concrete with sound markers, and the assessment explicitly satisfies the skill's step 4 by flagging thin premises (the father's work, the fifteen silent years).
- **Emotive: 9** — Emotionally literate throughout: "the sentence she fights for two acts and finally understands," and the unbearable symmetry of Bali's "I chose this."
- **Creativity: 8** — Strong conceits — fighting on the whales' terms, the ASI as the story's second spine — though several are shared with sibling entries.

### Entry G
- **Skill adherence: 8** — Complete structure, accurate markers, need visible at the checkpoints, and a specific weakest-beat verdict (the industrialists have no face or goal); prose runs slightly summary-flavored.
- **Emotive: 8** — "The kidnapping she crossed space to undo was never a kidnapping" and the doubled-abandonment framing give the middle real torque.
- **Creativity: 7** — Nice local color (a lunar dolphin tank, a humpback sea that refuses to move) but the reveals stay within the shared family.

### Entry I
- **Skill adherence: 9** — The fullest engagement with the skill: beats concrete, markers accurate, arc at all three checkpoints, and the assessment goes past spec by flagging two structural problems plus the skill's thin-premise list; runs longer than the target length.
- **Emotive: 10** — Best writing in the batch: "a word it cannot render at all," "the only sentence left to the one who wouldn't stay," and a mother who "stays put a moment longer than the house wants her to."
- **Creativity: 9** — "Everything that matters can walk" versus "not everything wants to" as the theme's two sides, the ASI as the argument for letting go, and a translator that renders nouns but fumbles feeling.

### Entry J
- **Skill adherence: 9** — Exemplary arc engineering: the mistranslated theme ("family stays together" versus "family survives distance") is corrected at Dark Night by the ASI, so the need is visible at every checkpoint; beats concrete, markers accurate.
- **Emotive: 9** — "Bali never said family stays together. She said family survives distance" lands emotionally; prose slightly more functional than the top two.
- **Creativity: 9** — The mistranslation device and the wormhole relay — the theme made the plot's working mechanism, authenticated publicly by whales in their own voices — are the family's most original mechanisms.

### Entry M
- **Skill adherence: 6** — All 15 beats named with markers, but Fun and Games ("Each planet is an adventure") is the exact generic placeholder the skill forbids, and Midpoint and Break into Three are skeletal.
- **Emotive: 7** — Plain prose with one genuinely affecting passage — the forced-abandonment realization — and a quietly moving final-image reversal.
- **Creativity: 8** — The inverted ending (she stays and puts down roots) and the hidden-protagonist misdirection are distinct choices no sibling makes, but the second-act middle is generic.
