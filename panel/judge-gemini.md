# Beat-Sheet Panel — Judge Gemini

> Research commentary is MIT licensed. Creative quotations and story expression are excluded; see [LICENSE](../LICENSE). Known evaluation errors are documented in the [audit](../RESULTS_AUDIT.md).

**What happened:** Evaluated and scored all 13 blind beat-sheet submissions (Entries A through M) for the *Whales* feature film project.  

**Date:** 2026-08-08

---

## Entry Scores & Evaluations

### Entry A
- **Score:** 8/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry A builds a clean, balanced 15-beat Save the Cat structure with strong thematic symmetry between the mother's wanderlust and the father's need for roots. The mid-act planetary travel sequence suffers slightly from episodic tourism, but the character arcs and structural bookends land cleanly.

### Entry B
- **Score:** 7/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry B delivers high-energy sci-fi set-pieces and dynamic action, but relies on a mechanical contrivance (orcas triggering a flood override) for the Act One escape. The third-act pivot to corporate mercenaries and armed haul tanks overshadows the story's intimate family resolution.

### Entry C
- **Score:** 8/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry C features exceptional thematic clarity around non-possessive love, culminating in a clever climax where real-time two-species translation converts habitat infrastructure into a trap. The transition into Break into Three is slightly compressed, but the structural self-assessment is thorough.

### Entry D
- **Score:** 9/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry D presents a highly disciplined 18-beat outline with strict cause-and-effect chaining and clear separation of internal and external character tracks. Its explicit mapping of open story dependencies and structural self-checks makes it a standout blueprint.

### Entry E
- **Score:** 8/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry E constructs a sophisticated dual-track engine that contrasts Nia's internal abandonment wound with the external search quest. While the summary formatting is somewhat dense, the thematic alignment and world-building rules are very well crafted.

### Entry F
- **Score:** 9/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry F excels through rigorous "therefore/but" causality and explicit state-change tracking across all three acts. It resolves key open narrative questions elegantly while anchoring the story in a poignant resolution of the orca matriline.

### Entry G
- **Score:** 7/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry G is an exceptionally readable and concise 15-beat breakdown that uses the communicator device effectively as both a narrative plant and an emotional bridge. However, its industrialist antagonists remain generic placeholders without clear motivation.

### Entry H
- **Score:** 10/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry H is a masterclass in story development that elevates the premise by defining low-frequency whale calls as generational duration and making Nia's own search the tracking beacon for the antagonists. With a 37-beat dual-track matrix, an Act Three redemption arc for the mother, and complete structural coherence, it is the definitive winner.

### Entry I
- **Score:** 8/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry I offers poetic prose and deeply resonant character moments, including a standout scene where the ASI challenges Nia's core assumptions. It leaves a noticeable gap in the mother's presence during Act Two, but its thematic self-awareness is top-notch.

### Entry J
- **Score:** 8/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry J features a stroke of brilliance in Beat 2, where Nia deliberately misinterprets Bali's call to fit her clingy view of family—a plant that pays off powerfully when the ASI exposes it. The corporate antagonists need more distinct characterization, but the character work is outstanding.

### Entry K
- **Score:** 7/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry K includes sharp thematic dialogue from the ASI and clear character motivations, but its climax strays into generic action-thriller territory with acoustic pulse weapons. The Act One breakout mechanism also feels slightly contrived.

### Entry L
- **Score:** 9/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry L provides a remarkably mature 115-minute sequence structure with rich world-building around the Listening Reef and acoustic wormhole navigation. The climax beat where Nia translates live while explicitly distinguishing her own words from Bali's is a magnificent dramatic turn.

### Entry M
- **Score:** 5/10
- **Reasoning:** Entry M scrambles the narrative momentum by discovering Bali at the Midpoint before revealing the father at All Is Lost, undercutting the central mystery engine. Furthermore, Nia's sudden decision in the final image to "put down roots" directly contradicts the story's core thematic resolution.
