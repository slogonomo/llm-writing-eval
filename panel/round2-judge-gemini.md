# Beat-Sheet Panel Round 2 Scoring — Gemini Judge

> Research commentary is MIT licensed. Creative quotations and story expression are excluded; see [LICENSE](../LICENSE). Known evaluation errors are documented in the [audit](../RESULTS_AUDIT.md).

**What happened:** Evaluated and scored the seven 15-beat story entries across skill compliance, emotive quality, and creativity.

**Date:** 2026-08-08

---

## Entry Evaluations

### Entry A
- **Skill Compliance:** 10/10 — Executes all 15 Blake Snyder beats with accurate page markers, concrete dramatic scenes, and a spot-on structural assessment identifying the specific vulnerability in Fun and Games.
- **Emotive Quality:** 9/10 — Captures a deeply moving thematic core around voluntary departure and family reconciliation, ending with a poignant matrilineal renewal.
- **Creativity:** 8/10 — Blends biobotic walking houses, sentient cetacean culture, and deep-space migration into a fresh emotional mystery about accepting chosen distance.

### Entry B
- **Skill Compliance:** 9/10 — Delivers a complete, well-structured 15-beat map with accurate page markers and an assessment that correctly flags the Break into Two plot contrivance.
- **Emotive Quality:** 7/10 — Functional and clear in its emotional beats, though the prose reads more like standard action-adventure plot notes than a deeply resonant character study.
- **Creativity:** 7/10 — Features good sci-fi elements like multi-species neural nets and space elevators, but relies heavily on familiar mercenary attack and compound-breach action tropes.

### Entry C
- **Skill Compliance:** 9.5/10 — Follows SKILL.md impeccably with full character input blocks, highly specific beat descriptions, and an incisive structural assessment of third-act setup needs.
- **Emotive Quality:** 9.5/10 — Written with evocative prose and lyrical precision, making emotional turns like the father reveal and the lesson of "love that doesn't chain" land with narrative weight.
- **Creativity:** 8.5/10 — Excellent thematic integration of inter-species communication and non-violent acoustic strategy against corporate exploitation.

### Entry G
- **Skill Compliance:** 9/10 — Strictly adheres to the 15-beat structure and page markers while providing a solid structural assessment of the faceless antagonist in Bad Guys Close In.
- **Emotive Quality:** 8.5/10 — Builds strong emotional torque around the doubled abandonment by father and matriarch, resolving elegantly in the Final Image.
- **Creativity:** 7.5/10 — Offers fun world-building details such as lunar dolphin tanks and humpback seas, but adheres closely to the central narrative template.

### Entry I
- **Skill Compliance:** 9.5/10 — Comprehensive compliance that goes beyond the basics to audit thin beats and structural gaps per Step 4 of the skill, though slightly exceeding nominal length.
- **Emotive Quality:** 10/10 — A masterclass in prose and emotional texture, using brilliant phrasing ("echolocating a hole in the world") to elevate the beat sheet into literature.
- **Creativity:** 9/10 — Exceptionally original conceptual framing that treats the ASI as a guide of pedagogical restraint and turns language barriers into core dramatic obstacles.

### Entry J
- **Skill Compliance:** 9/10 — Fully compliant with Blake Snyder's 15 beats and page allocations, paired with an accurate assessment of the antagonist's commercial motivation gap.
- **Emotive Quality:** 8/10 — Clear and emotionally coherent, using a clever Theme Stated mistranslation ("family stays together" vs "family survives distance") to anchor Nia's growth.
- **Creativity:** 8.5/10 — Highly inventive use of the thematic mistranslation device and a well-conceived climax centered on a multi-world acoustic relay array.

### Entry M
- **Skill Compliance:** 5/10 — Severely misplaces key structural beats by inverting the Midpoint false victory and All Is Lost reveal, while falling short of the required word count.
- **Emotive Quality:** 4/10 — Written in flat, summary-heavy prose ("Each planet is an adventure") that fails to generate emotional connection or narrative tension.
- **Creativity:** 4/10 — Relies on generic tropes and ends on an unmotivated resolution where Nia abruptly decides to stay behind while her companion leaves.
