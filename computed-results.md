## Counting and interpretation

Original word counts are whitespace-delimited counts of the complete blinded files before publication metadata cleanup (equivalent to `wc -w`); headings and assessments are included. They are stored in `entries.csv` so metadata edits do not silently change historical correlations. Correlations and ranks use unrounded scores. Spearman uses average ranks for score ties, not the judges' separate prose tie-breaks.

These are descriptive associations from one story and a small, related set of entries. They do not establish a causal length effect, population-wide model rankings, or a statistically tested absence of self-preference.
