# Feature Film Beat Sheet: Whales

## Story Engine
- **Protagonist & Core Drive**: 15-year-old Nia, a nomadic whale-talker raised in a biobotic walking house in the Pacific Northwest (year 2109), uses neural-cetacean translation tech to communicate with her adopted orca pod. When the pod's beloved matriarch Bali is abducted, Nia defies her pragmatic mother and launches into deep space to find Bali and say the goodbye she was denied when her father vanished years ago.
- **Central Conflict & Structural Symmetry**: Nia believes she is hunting a ruthless off-world animal smuggler, only to discover the captor is her long-lost father—and that Bali contacted *him* to voluntarily join his stationary scientific research on galactic cetacean consciousness. Nia must face the painful symmetry that both her human father and her whale matriarch chose a higher purpose over staying with her.
- **Stakes & Cost**: When industrialist mercenaries track Nia's search to her father's hidden ocean sanctuary to steal his life's work, Nia must unite with her father, her hacker friend, and a pedagogical ASI to defend the sanctuary, heal her familial trauma, and master translation to bridge Earth's pod with Bali across the stars.
- **Thematic Axis**: True hurt comes from those we love, and holding on out of resentment pulls us underwater like a bad tide. Saying goodbye with true understanding allows families—both born and adopted—to stay connected across light-years.

## Beat List
- **1. Opening Image (Pacific Northwest, 2109)**: Nia (15) swims effortlessly in cold coastal waters alongside Bali, the matriarch orca of her adopted pod. Nia fine-tunes her neural-translation harness, exchanging concepts with Bali. On shore, Nia's mother pilots their biobotic walking house along the cliffs. In a post-Awakening world where learning and tech are integrated into daily life, Nia's community thrives in constant motion, though Nia harbors a quiet void left by her missing father.
- **2. Theme Stated**: During a morning lesson on pod migration, Nia's mother observes: "Some things are meant to keep moving. Holding onto what wants to drift only drowns you." Nia pushes back; she refuses to view departure as erasure, still stung by her father leaving without saying goodbye when she was four.
- **3. Set-Up & Inciting Incident (The Abduction)**: Disturbed pod members approach Nia—Bali has vanished, undetectable by long-range echolocation. Forced embedded tracking chips were outlawed on Earth in 2098, leaving no signal. Nia enlists her friend, an enhanced techie outside her walking community, who hacks orbital feeds to reveal satellite footage: a trans-orbital tanker lowering a containment harness into the ocean, lifting Bali into the sky.
- **4. Debate (Friction & Refusal)**: Nia confronts her mother, demanding they mount a chase into space. Her mother refuses: "There's nothing we can do. People, and whales, just have to go." Furious and hurt, Nia declares: "Not without saying goodbye." Nia secretly gathers gear to reach the space elevator port. Her mother discovers her packing and locks down the biobotic house's movement protocols to confine her.
- **5. Break into Two / Lock-in (Escape to the Stars)**: The orca pod gathers near the shore, sending coordinated low-frequency acoustic pulses that trick the walking house's environmental sensors into overriding the lockdown. Nia and her hacker friend escape to the space elevator hub. Before stepping into the lift cab, Nia leaves an acoustic sub-space communicator with the pod, promising to find Bali.
- **6. B-Story & Companion Entry (The Pedagogical ASI)**: En route to orbital transit, Nia is intercepted by her family's embodied ASI tutor—a witty, hyper-intelligent droid dispatched by her mother. Bound by strict educational protocols, the ASI cannot directly solve Nia's problems, forcing her to lead while the droid provides sharp commentary and steps in only when fatal danger looms.
- **7. Fun and Games (Wormholes & Ocean Worlds)**: Nia, her techie friend, and the ASI contract a grizzled, Jaws-esque spacer who navigates wormhole routes to alien ocean worlds. They chase leads across aquatic outposts, encountering space-faring dolphins and humpback sanctuaries. Nia refines her translation harness while building a profile of the mysterious scientist piloting the tanker.
- **8. Midpoint Turn (The Father's Footprint)**: Their search leads to an uncharted water world. Scouting a remote coastline, Nia is shocked to find a biobotic walking house identical to her mother's design. Inside the vacant structure, Nia finds holographic archives: old photos of her mother, Bali, and a photo of four-year-old Nia held by her father. Suddenly, security protocols engage—the house traps them inside and marches inland toward an imposing coastal research citadel.
- **9. Bad Guys Close In (Escalation & Shadow Pursuers)**: The hacker friend overrides the house's lock in the nick of time as it enters the citadel perimeter. Meanwhile, industrialist mercenaries—having tracked Nia's orbital query trail—enter the star system to seize the facility. Nia storms into the flooded sanctuary dome, confronting her father in fury, believing he abducted Bali just as he abandoned her family.
- **10. All Hope Lost / Crisis (The Truth of Bali & Father)**: Nia reaches Bali's containment pool, but when she connects the translation harness, Bali reveals the truth: *Bali contacted him*. Faced with her pod line splitting on Earth, Bali chose to join Nia's father for his deep-space cetacean resonance project—research requiring a stationary planet base to establish trans-galactic communion. Nia's father explains he left not to abandon Nia, but because his work required sitting still while her mother required perpetual movement. The realization that both her father and matriarch voluntarily left stabs Nia to her core.
- **11. Dark Night of the Soul**: Industrialist gunships breach the system, jamming sub-space channels and threatening to destroy the sanctuary if the research isn't surrendered. Sitting by the pool, feeling twice-abandoned, Nia despairs. Her ASI tutor gently relaxes its constraints: "Understanding why someone leaves doesn't erase the wound, Nia. But holding onto anger hides the bridge they left open for you."
- **12. Break into Three (Rallying the Sanctuary)**: Nia realizes her unique bond with Bali and her custom translation harness are the missing keys to activating her father's planet-wide acoustic array. She reconciles with her father, uniting their team—hacker, ASI, old spacer, and scientist—to protect Bali and defend the research.
- **13. Climax (The Battle for the Sanctuary)**: As mercenary breachers drop into the sanctuary, Nia dives into the central resonance pool with Bali, aligning her translation harness with the main transducer array. Her hacker friend and the ASI disrupt the mercenaries' targeting systems while the old spacer engages troop ships in the sky. Nia and Bali project a massive acoustic pulse through the wormhole relay, summoning local space-dolphins and allied spacers to disable the mercenary fleet and secure the sanctuary.
- **14. Resolution (The Goodbye & Reconnection)**: With the industrialists defeated and the sanctuary safe, Nia and her father share a deep, emotional reconciliation. Though invited to stay, Nia chooses to return to Earth to serve as the bridge between communities. Bali sends a heartfelt translated message of love and guidance back to her pod.
- **15. Ending Image**: Back in the Pacific Northwest, Nia stands before her pod at the water's edge. Using her upgraded translation system, she delivers a moving speech to the young orcas and her mother: "Sometimes people, and whales, just have to go. But that doesn't mean they're gone." She activates the sub-space communicator—and Bali's voice echoes warmly through the Pacific waters from light-years away.
