***Blake Snyder Beat Sheet (BS2)***

**Opening Image** (p.1) – Nia swims in the coastal waters. Several orcas swim around her, making whale sounds that she sees translated into English on the HUD in her contact lenses. One of the whales, the matriarch Bali, seems furtive, concerned. Nia calms her quietly.

**Set-up** (pp. 1-10) – We see Nia’s life: the nomadic village she lives in. Walking houses, integrated technology that is almost magical. Chairs put themselves away, windows turn to screens, and no one notices. This is commonplace. Children follow her when she returns, asking if the whales told her any good stories today.

**Theme Stated** (p. 6\) – Mara says, over coffee, “they came back early.” Nia says, “somehow I’m never sure they’ll come back at all.” Her mother replies, “Someday they won’t, or you won’t. But you’ll keep moving, it’s what we do.” Nia thinks of her father. This is the choice she’ll have to make for herself.

**Catalyst** (p. 12\) – A member of the pod tells Nia that the matriarch is missing. When Nia can’t find her, she seeks the help of Cal. Together they find footage showing a large ship scooping her up and taking her off-world.

**Debate** (p.12-25) – She brings it to her mother, asks for help. Her mother refuses, and says “sometimes people just have to go.” Mara forbids Nia from pursuing the whale and her captors, and tries to prevent Nia from leaving.

**Break Into Two** (p. 25\) – She can’t let her go, not without at least saying goodbye. With the help of Cal and the orca’s granddaughters, she evades her mother and seeks passage to space. Nia promises “we’ll bring her home.”

**B Story** (p.27) – She tells Cal that she can’t do it alone. She’s never been off-world, and he knows his way around. He agrees to take her as far as the spaceport.

**Fun and Games** (pp. 30 \- 60\)  – Nia assembles a band of adventurers, talking Cal into joining her, and adding an old space captain and the unhelpful droid her mom sent after her. They journey from planet to planet, talking to the whales and the people, picking up the trail and closing in. Each planet is an adventure.

**Midpoint** (pp. 60\) – Bali, found. The friends celebrate, drawing closer and feeling on top of the world. They rest tonight, and tomorrow, they head to their final location, planning to bring the matriarch home to her children.

**Bad Guys Close In** (pp. 60-65) – As the friends take off, the audience sees that the band is being observed, stalked, followed. The kidnapper is onto them, and they are completely unaware.

**All is Lost** (pp. 65-75) – They land at their destination. Nia is surprised to see a walking house here, so similar to those of her tribe. The houses are unique \- it shouldn’t be here. She enters, and finds pictures of her mother, then finally of her as a little girl, her mother, and HER FATHER. She is in her father’s house, and he is the kidnapper.

**Dark Night of the Soul** (pp. 75-85) – When she confronts her father, she finds out that Bali contacted him, that she chose to leave Earth and to help her father with his work. She realizes that she’ll be forced to tell her granddaughters that they’ve been abandoned, and she knows the hurt of that far too well.

**Break Into Three** (p. 85\) – The band starts to leave, to head home, and they see a hidden force moving toward her father and the whale. The audience realizes the bad guys they thought worked for her father are actually out to get him. Cal says “we’ve gotta help them.” Nia agrees.

**Finale** (pp. 85-105) – Her father and Bali must leave, disappear again, to finish their work. Nia helps them escape, but slips a communicator to Bali to use “when it’s safe” so her granddaughters don’t have to suffer the same abandonment Nia did.

**Final Image** (p. 110\) – Nia and Cal are on the beach. Cal says “I know you’re all leaving tomorrow. I guess this is goodbye.”

“It isn’t,” Nia says. “I’ve decided to stay here for this cycle. To put down some roots.”

**Structural Assessment**: The architecture is strong. The Midpoint joy and the following reveal have great contrast. The challenge is introducing and hinting at the hidden protagonist earlier in the story. He needs development as well to not feel like a faceless menace. The other challenge is explaining the dark night of the soul, and the motivation behind her father and Bali’s independent migrations.
